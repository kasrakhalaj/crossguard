MOSSLIGHT — LEARNING EDITION

Double-click PLAY.cmd to play in a window, or run Mosslight.exe directly.
Keep all files in this folder together.

Enter: begin
A/D or arrows: move
Space: jump (hold for a higher jump)
J or left click: strike
Shift or K: dash
E: rest at a lantern
Escape: pause, sound/shake settings, or return to checkpoint

Gamepad: left stick, bottom face button to jump, left face button to
strike, right bumper to dash, top face button to rest, Start to pause.

Find the Bellkeeper, defeat it, then continue right to the dawn bell.
Lanterns are checkpoints. There is no save between sessions.

Open this Unity project and choose Mosslight > Open Learning Scene
to inspect the game. The map appears when you press Play.

Learning guide: Assets/Mosslight/START_HERE.md in the project folder.
First exercise: open Assets/Mosslight/Settings/LearningTuning.asset,
change Jump Speed from 13.5 to 15, and compare the jump.

Original geometric artwork and synthesized audio. A small learning
prototype with simple animation, not a finished commercial release.
