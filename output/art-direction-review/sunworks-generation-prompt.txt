Use case: stylized-concept. One in-game composition concept for an original completely 2D SIDE-SCROLLING pixel-art knight game, working title Crossguard / Light Knight. NO lettering, no title, no UI, no split panels. Landscape 16:9. Show the proposed location "The Last Sunworks": an abandoned fortified workshop built into a rain-soaked sea cliff where craftsmen once captured daylight in thick amber glass and brass reflectors. Build a convincing sense of depth using only painted flat 2D sprite layers. Camera strictly side-on, playable horizontal walkway across lower third; NOT isometric, NOT top-down, NOT 3D render. At left foreground a thick cropped near-black arch with a hanging cable frames but does not block hero; foreground masonry has clear thickness and bevelled top lips. At x roughly 30% a small original closed-helmet armored knight, square amber visor and small wine-red scarf, equipped with readable sword and shield, stands on wet pale slate floor, lit by a small surviving amber service lantern. Knight height roughly 15% of frame. Mid-ground center a broken brass reflector leaning in its frame and shelves holding unlit amber glass cylinders, drained glass fragments near a warm workbench. At right on the same gameplay plane a sealed heavy workshop door has three amber rivets and a narrow warm leak under it, inviting curiosity about what still operates inside; a brass conduit connects it to the abandoned apparatus. Door not colossal, credible reused architecture. Behind the walkway, a window opening shows sheer depth: broken aqueduct / service bridges receding in 3 overlapping layers, distant cliff fortress and huge dead sun-collector dish in low-contrast blue-grey rain haze. Deep drop behind the walkway, all depth is scenery only; no receding playable route. Rain only outdoors; inside has a few airborne dust particles, no fog curtain across the knight. Strong clear shapes, large quiet surfaces, sparse meaningful prop placement, no repetitive random crates or fantasy runes. Pixelish aesthetic with crisp deliberate square pixel clusters and limited detail, like a 384x216 source scaled up by nearest-neighbor. Keep cold damp slate, blue steel, tarnished bronze, amber glass and burgundy cloth. Warm light only occupies a small part of scene; focal brightness knight visor, lantern, door slit. Physical light direction coherent from warm lantern and cool exterior; tight contact shadows anchor feet and props, cast shadows run away from lantern, bronze catches thin highlights, wood stays matte. No excessive bloom, painterly smudging, glossy toy materials, gothic cathedral arches, red crosses, horned insect hero or skulls. Mood: lonely, tactile, quietly mysterious, a workplace recently abandoned rather than generic medieval dungeon. This is a visual target, not a finished asset atlas.
